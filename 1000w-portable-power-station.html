1000W FAQ

Portable power station questions

What can a 1000W portable power station run?▼
A 1000W station can run suitable appliances below its continuous output rating, but motor, compressor and heating products can require a higher starting surge. Always compare the appliance label and surge requirement with the station specification.
How long will a 1100Wh battery last?▼
A rough ideal calculation is capacity divided by load: about 11 hours at 100W, 3.6 hours at 300W, 2.2 hours at 500W or 1.4 hours at 800W. Real runtime is shorter after inverter losses, battery reserve, temperature and appliance cycling.
Which 1000W models are available?▼
The catalogue includes M1000-01 and P1000-01, both listed at 1000W output with 1100Wh capacity. Case design and model positioning differ, so compare the current product specification before choosing.
Can the station be charged with solar panels?▼
Folding solar panels are available, but panel voltage, connector, solar input limit and charging current must match the selected model. The compatible configuration is confirmed in the quotation.
Do you offer wholesale and OEM programs?▼
Yes. Distributor and private-label programs can include model selection, branding, packaging, manuals, plug standards and shipment planning, subject to model and order quantity.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Available documents are confirmed before the order, while destination clearance remains the importer's responsibility.
Can you ship a 1000W power station to South Africa or another African market?▼
Bulk and sample shipments can be quoted for the destination port or city. Battery shipping restrictions, carton dimensions and the final delivery route are confirmed before the order.
