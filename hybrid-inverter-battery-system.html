Buyer FAQ

Hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery questions

What is a hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery system?▼
It combines a hybrid inverter, LiFePO4 battery storage, solar input, grid or generator input and project monitoring. The inverter manages when power comes from solar, battery, grid or generator according to the site design.
How do I size a hybrid inverter and battery system?▼
Start with the appliance load list, total running watts, motor or compressor surge, daily energy use, required backup hours and available solar input. Those inputs determine the inverter rating, battery capacity and solar array.
Can the system work with a diesel generator?▼
Generator integration can be reviewed as part of the system design. The inverter, generator capacity, changeover arrangement and site wiring must be checked together by the project electrician.
Do you supply complete systems or individual components?▼
Both project packages and individual hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries, solar panels and accessories can be reviewed, depending on the destination, quantity and technical scope.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Available certificates and test reports are confirmed before quotation, and destination approval remains project-specific.
Can the system be installed off-grid?▼
Off-grid operation can be designed around solar, battery and generator input. The final architecture depends on the load profile, location, backup requirement and whether utility power is available.
What information is needed for a quotation?▼
Send the country, site type, appliance load list, running and surge watts, daily energy use, backup hours, solar space, generator details and expected quantity.
