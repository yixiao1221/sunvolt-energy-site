Sample Evaluation

What to Test Before a Bulk Order

Use the sample period to verify the features that matter to your customers. Check charging speed from the mains and a solar panel, output stability with representative loads, port fit, fan noise, display accuracy, housing finish, and the clarity of labels and instructions. Test battery runtime with realistic equipment rather than relying only on the catalog capacity.

Distributors should also inspect the retail box, accessories, protection settings, and startup behavior after storage or transport. Record any issue with photos, videos, load details, and the model serial number so our team can respond quickly. If the sample meets expectations, include the approved specification and packaging version in the bulk-order confirmation.
