Who We Are

SunVolt Energy is a Foshan-based supplier specializing in portable power stations, solar generators, and energy storage solutions. We partner with ISO 9001 and CE-certified manufacturers to deliver reliable, affordable power products to markets worldwide.

Founded by a team with 15+ years of experience in the building materials and energy product export industry, we understand the quality standards and service expectations of international B2B buyers.

Our mission: Make reliable backup power accessible and affordable for everyone, especially in regions where electricity is not always available.

Factory Partnership

Built for Repeat B2B Supply

SunVolt Energy supports importers, distributors, installers, and private-label brands with a production process built for repeat orders. Our team reviews the target market, required output, battery type, packaging, and certification needs before quoting. Standard models can ship quickly, while OEM projects follow sampling, artwork approval, trial production, and final inspection steps.

Every order includes an agreed product specification, quantity schedule, export packing standard, and shipment plan. Before delivery, we check appearance, function, charging, output, protection features, and accessories. Buyers receive shipment photos and tracking information, and our sales team remains available after arrival for technical questions, warranty claims, and reorder planning.
