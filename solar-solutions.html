Project FAQ

Integrated Solar Storage Questions

📏

What Size Do I Need?

System size depends on daily consumption, peak load, roof area, and required backup hours. Villa systems commonly start at 5-20 kWh; commercial projects can scale from 30 kWh to 1 MWh+.

🔌

Can It Work Off-Grid?

Yes. The hybrid inverter can coordinate solar, battery, grid, and generator input, so the same platform supports grid-tied hybrid and off-grid projects.

🛠

Do You Design the System?

Send your equipment list, daily usage, destination country, and installation photos. We return a system design, equipment list, and quotation.

📦

What Is Included?

Typical packages include solar panels, hybrid inverter, LiFePO4 battery modules, racking or mounting accessories, monitoring, and installation documents.

Need a Custom Solar Storage System?

Send us your daily load and installation photos. We will design a complete system and return a quotation within 24 hours.
