Application Planning

Choose Capacity from Real Loads

Portable power stations are easiest to size when the buyer lists each device, its running watts, starting watts, and daily operating hours. Lighting, routers, phones, fans, and laptops usually allow long runtime from a compact model. Refrigerators, pumps, power tools, and air-conditioning require a larger inverter and a higher surge rating, even when their average energy use looks modest.

For shops and clinics, prioritize the loads that must remain online during an outage. For events and field work, consider transport, charging time, and solar input. Send your load list to our team and we will recommend a practical power-station class or an integrated solar storage system with an appropriate battery margin.
