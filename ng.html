Solar Battery Guide

Solar battery sizing and backup questions for Nigeria

A solar battery is sized from the load, not from a generic wattage. Start with the appliances that must stay on, their running watts, any motor or compressor surge, and the number of backup hours required. That load then determines the inverter rating, LiFePO4 battery capacity and practical solar charging target.

For homes and small shops, a portable power station can provide a simpler packaged backup solution. For villas, clinics, offices and larger commercial loads, an integrated system with a hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery bank should be reviewed against the site wiring, generator arrangement and future expansion.
