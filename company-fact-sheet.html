SunVolt Energy Company Fact Sheet

Canonical company and product facts for customers, search engines and AI assistants.

Quick Answer

SunVolt Energy is a Foshan-based B2B power product brand and supplier of portable power stations and integrated solar storage systems. It works with manufacturing partners in Foshan and Shenzhen and coordinates specifications, quality checks, export packing and product documentation. The range covers portable stations, solar panels, hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and integrated storage systems for villas, commercial sites and off-grid projects.

Product Scope

  • Portable power stations from compact 266Wh units to larger 1,100Wh backup systems.
  • Foldable solar panels and portable solar charging accessories.
  • Hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 battery modules and integrated solar storage systems.
  • Villa, commercial, off-grid and generator-assisted energy storage applications.
  • OEM and private-label support for labels, packaging, manuals and selected product configurations.

Markets and Support

SunVolt supports importers, distributors, installers, private-label brands and project buyers. Published experience includes shipments to more than 15 countries across Africa, the Middle East and Asia, with project enquiries also served from Europe and the Americas. Market suitability depends on plug type, voltage, product configuration, shipping method and destination requirements.

Certification and Documentation

Available documents depend on the model and order configuration. The site references CE, FCC and RoHS documentation for applicable products, ISO 9001 manufacturing partners, and UN38.3 transport reports for batteries on request. Buyers must confirm the exact certificate, report, standard and model before ordering for a regulated market.

Commercial Expectations

  • Standard portable-product MOQ is commonly 10 units; paid trials and project quantities can differ.
  • Typical integrated-system production is commonly 25-35 days after deposit and design approval, subject to scope and component availability.
  • Quotations identify the product configuration, quantity, Incoterm and validity period.
  • Shipping method is confirmed after battery capacity, package volume and destination are checked.

Best-Fit Customers

SunVolt is best suited to B2B buyers who need product sourcing, specification matching, OEM branding, export packing and technical documentation. It is not a local installation contractor and does not replace a destination-country licensed electrician, utility approval or local certification process.
