Buyer Checklist

What to confirm before selecting a UAE system

Appliance load

Running watts, starting surge, operating hours and whether the full site or only essential circuits will be backed up.

Heat and ventilation

Battery placement, shade, airflow, ambient temperature and charging limits must suit the installation location.

Local installation

Grid connection, permits, authority inspection and commissioning must be handled by the importer and an appropriately licensed local contractor.

Import route

Quantity, destination city or port, product documentation, plug type and the importer's clearance arrangement.

Approval Boundary

Product supply does not replace local approval

SunVolt Energy supplies products and export documents from Foshan, China. We do not claim a UAE factory, local warehouse or licensed installation service. Grid-connected systems, electrical permits, product registration and authority inspection vary by emirate and remain the responsibility of the importer and its appointed local contractor.

Model-specific CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documents can be reviewed before quotation where available. The destination importer must confirm which documents the relevant UAE authority requires for the exact model and installation.

UAE FAQ

Portable power and solar storage questions

Which portable power station is suitable for the UAE?▼
A 300W class suits basic devices, a 500W class fits many apartments and small shops, and a 1000W class may suit larger retail, clinic or project loads. Selection depends on running watts, starting surge, backup hours and heat exposure.
Can you supply hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery systems for the UAE?▼
Integrated solar storage can be reviewed with hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and solar panels after checking the daily load, backup duration, available installation space and existing electrical arrangement.
Do you ship to Dubai and Abu Dhabi?▼
Export shipments can be quoted to Jebel Ali or another selected UAE port or destination. The freight route, battery shipping restrictions and final delivery are confirmed before the order.
Does SunVolt Energy provide UAE installation or authority approval?▼
No. We supply products and export documentation from China. Grid connection, electrical permits, product registration and installation vary by emirate and must be handled by the importer and an appropriately licensed local contractor.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Available product and export documents are confirmed before quotation. UAE registration and authority requirements remain the importer's responsibility.
Do you offer wholesale and OEM supply for UAE distributors?▼
Distributors and private-label buyers can review model selection, branding, packaging, manuals, plug standards and shipment planning, subject to the model and order quantity.
What should I send for a UAE quotation?▼
Send the destination city or port, quantity, appliance load, required output or battery capacity, backup hours, solar requirement, plug standard, installation arrangement and expected delivery date.
