Buyer Checklist

What to confirm before selecting a system

Appliance load

Running watts, starting surge, operating hours and whether the full site or only essential circuits will be backed up.

Solar and backup

Daily energy use, required outage duration, available solar area, generator use and future expansion.

Installation

Plug type, fixed or portable arrangement, battery ventilation and the local electrician's responsibility.

Import route

Quantity, destination city or port, product documentation and the importer's clearance arrangement.

Kenya FAQ

Portable power and solar storage questions

Which portable power station is suitable for Kenya?▼
A 300W class suits basic devices, a 500W class fits many homes and small shops, and a 1000W class may suit larger shops, clinics or higher-surge equipment. Selection depends on the appliance load and required backup time.
Can you supply solar battery and hybrid inverter systems for Kenya?▼
Integrated solar storage can be reviewed with hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and solar panels after checking the daily load, backup hours, solar space and existing electrical arrangement.
Do you ship to Nairobi and Mombasa?▼
Export shipments can be quoted to the selected Kenyan port or destination city. The freight route, battery shipping restrictions and final delivery are confirmed before the order.
Are portable power stations suitable for off-grid sites?▼
Portable stations can support suitable plug-in loads at remote sites. Larger off-grid projects should use an integrated system sized around the daily load, solar resource and generator support.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Available product and export documents are confirmed before quotation, while Kenyan clearance remains the importer's responsibility.
Do you offer wholesale and OEM supply for Kenyan distributors?▼
Distributors and private-label buyers can review model selection, branding, packaging, manuals, plug standards and shipment planning, subject to the model and order quantity.
What should I send for a Kenya quotation?▼
Send the destination city or port, quantity, appliance load, required output or battery capacity, backup hours, solar requirement, plug standard and expected delivery date.
