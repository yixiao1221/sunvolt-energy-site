South Africa FAQ

Portable power and solar battery questions

Which portable power station is best for South Africa?▼
A 300W class suits basic devices and short backup, a 500W class fits many homes and small shops, and a 1000W class suits larger shops, clinics or appliances with higher surge demand. Selection depends on the appliance load and required runtime.
Is storage still useful if load shedding has reduced?▼
Storage can still be reviewed for peak tariffs, solar self-consumption, local outages, diesel avoidance and business continuity. The economic case depends on the site tariff, load profile and operating pattern.
Can you supply hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery systems?▼
Integrated systems can be reviewed with hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and solar panels after checking the daily load, required backup, solar space and existing electrical arrangement.
Do you ship to Cape Town, Durban or Johannesburg?▼
Export shipments can be quoted to the selected South African port or destination city. The freight route, battery shipping restrictions and final delivery are confirmed before the order.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Available product and export documents are confirmed before quotation, while South African approval and clearance remain project-specific.
Do you offer wholesale and OEM supply for South African distributors?▼
Distributors and private-label buyers can review model selection, branding, packaging, manuals, plug standards and shipment planning, subject to the model and order quantity.
What should I send for a South Africa quotation?▼
Send the destination city or port, quantity, appliance load, required output or battery capacity, backup hours, solar requirement, plug standard and expected delivery date.
