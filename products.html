Buyer FAQ

Portable power station pricing and supply questions

Are the displayed prices retail or wholesale?▼
The main product-card prices are B2B FOB Shenzhen reference prices starting from MOQ 20+. Sample EXW prices are shown separately and do not include freight or destination charges.
Can SunVolt Energy customize a portable power station for our brand?▼
OEM and private-label options can include model selection, branding, plug standard, packaging and user manuals, subject to the selected model and order quantity.
Which portable power station sizes are available?▼
The catalogue covers 300W, 400W, 500W, 600W, 700W, 800W and 1000W output classes, with battery capacities from 166.5Wh to 1100Wh, plus folding solar panels and solar bags.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the product model and configuration. Available documents are confirmed for the selected product and destination before ordering.
Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?▼
Paid trial orders are available for selected portable power station models. Sample product prices and freight are quoted separately by model and destination.
