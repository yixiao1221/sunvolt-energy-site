Sales Process

What Happens After You Contact Us

1. Requirement Review

We check your products, quantity, destination, target price, and expected delivery date. If the application is technical, we also review load data, solar input, battery autonomy, or installation conditions so the recommendation fits the project rather than only the catalog.

2. Quotation and Samples

You receive a clear quotation with model, specification, quantity tier, Incoterm, lead time, and payment terms. Buyers who need product validation can order one or two sample units before a bulk purchase. OEM customers can discuss logo, packaging, manual, and carton requirements.

3. Production and Delivery

After confirmation, we schedule production, share progress updates, and prepare export documents. Before shipment we confirm packing, markings, and logistics details. Final tracking information is sent when the goods leave the factory so your team can plan customs clearance and local delivery.

To speed up the first reply, include the model or capacity you are considering, estimated order quantity, destination country, and whether you need standard branding or private-label production. For integrated solar systems, attach a load list or equipment schedule if one is available.

Our normal response time is within two hours during working hours. WhatsApp is the fastest channel for pricing, product photos, videos, and document requests. Email is suitable for formal quotations, purchase-order documents, and technical files that need to be stored by your procurement team.

Please send one message with all key details instead of splitting the request across several channels. This allows the same sales manager to keep the quotation, specification, and shipment terms consistent. If another colleague will handle purchasing or logistics, include their contact information and preferred language. For after-sales support, send the original order number, model, serial number, and a short video showing the issue so our technical team can identify the correct next step.
