Solar Battery Guide

Solar battery sizing and backup questions for Saudi Arabia

For a Saudi villa or commercial site, start with the appliances that must stay on during an outage. Add the running watts, motor or compressor surge, required backup hours, available generator support and space for ventilation. Those inputs determine the hybrid inverter rating, LiFePO4 battery capacity and practical solar charging target.

Portable power stations are straightforward for selected plug-in loads and temporary sites. Larger villa, clinic, office or commercial loads should be reviewed as an integrated system with proper circuit allocation, battery ventilation and installation by a qualified electrical contractor.

What size solar battery do I need for a Saudi villa?▼
Capacity depends on the appliances that must remain powered, running watts, compressor or motor surge and required backup hours. Send the load list and backup target so the inverter, LiFePO4 battery and solar input can be sized together.
Do you supply hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery systems?▼
Integrated solar storage can be coordinated with hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and solar panels after reviewing the site load, generator arrangement, available space and required backup duration.
