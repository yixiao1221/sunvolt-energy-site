Africa FAQ

Portable power and solar storage questions

Which portable power station size is suitable for Africa?▼
A 300W class suits basic lighting and small devices, a 500W class fits many homes and small shops, and a 1000W class may suit larger shops, clinics or higher-surge equipment. Final selection depends on the appliance load and backup hours.
Can you supply hybrid inverter and LiFePO4 battery systems for Africa?▼
Integrated solar storage can be reviewed for homes, commercial sites and off-grid projects using hybrid inverters, LiFePO4 batteries and solar panels sized around the site load.
Do you offer wholesale and OEM supply for African distributors?▼
Distributors and private-label buyers can review model selection, branding, packaging, manuals, plug standards and shipment planning, subject to the model and order quantity.
Which African markets do you support?▼
The site provides market guidance for Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa, and we can review other African destinations by port, product mix and importer requirements.
Which certification documents are available?▼
CE, FCC, RoHS and UN38.3 documentation depends on the selected model and configuration. Export and product documents are confirmed before the order, while destination-specific clearance remains the importer's responsibility.
Can you help calculate the landed product mix?▼
We can review product cost, carton dimensions, quantity and the selected shipping route. Import duty, clearing and local delivery should be confirmed with the importer's freight agent in the destination country.
What information is needed for an Africa quotation?▼
Send the country, destination city or port, quantity, required output or battery capacity, solar requirement, plug standard, packaging needs and expected delivery date.
